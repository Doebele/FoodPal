Privacy

11 September 2026

The short version

Cafcalog has no account, no server and no measurement tools. What you log stays on your device. Three things leave it — and each one only because you asked for it.

What stays on the device

Your entries — name, time, calories, caffeine, nutrients — and the photos you take or choose. Your settings too. All of it is stored in the app itself. Nothing is sent to me, nothing syncs through iCloud, and there is no backup beyond the one your device makes anyway. Delete the app and this data is gone.

Apple Health

With the switch in settings turned on, Cafcalog writes calories, caffeine, protein, carbohydrates and fat to Apple Health. That happens on the device; I see none of it. Nothing is read — the app never asks Health for data, it only files it. Delete an entry in Cafcalog and the matching Health entry goes with it.

What leaves the device

1. The estimate from a photo or a description

To estimate, the image or your text goes to the service you picked in settings — Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral or one of the others. From that moment their privacy terms govern that transfer; which service it is, is your choice.

  • Pick Apple and your device does the work itself. Then nothing leaves it.
  • Pick a model on your own network (LM Studio on your Mac) and the request goes there only — across your Wi-Fi, not the internet.

2. Packaged food

Looking up a packaged product asks Open Food Facts. What travels is the barcode or the search term. The request identifies itself with the app's project address — no mail address, no device identifier.

3. Dictation

Dictation uses Apple's speech recognition. Where your device can do it, it recognises on device; where it cannot, Apple's servers take over. Cafcalog stores no recordings — only the text left in the field.

API keys

Keys for the services live in your device's keychain, not in the app's settings file. They leave the device only as a header on the request to the very service they belong to.

What never happens

  • no analytics, no statistics, no measurement of how you use the app
  • no advertising, no tracking, no identifier shared across apps
  • no sharing with third parties, no selling, no profile
  • no third-party components that phone home

Permissions

  • Camera — only when you take a photo.
  • Photos — only for the image you choose.
  • Microphone and speech recognition — only for dictation.
  • Apple Health — write only, and only with your yes.

You can withdraw any of these in system settings at any time. The app keeps working; only that one path closes.

Your rights

Access, correction, deletion: the data is yours and it is with you. Edit and delete entries in the app; remove everything else by deleting the app. I hold no copy that I could hand over or erase.

Changes

If the app changes something written here, this page changes with it. The date at the top says when it last did.
