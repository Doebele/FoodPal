The thumb decides

Cafcalog is held in one hand — and its controls follow the hand that holds it. Capture, your most frequent coffee, the close button: everything the thumb should reach moves into its corner.

Right or left is a setting, changeable any time, no restart. The timeline itself still reads left to right; a curve stays a curve, whichever hand holds it.

Three numeral styles, one counter

The day's total is not a number in a line but a display: flip cards after the Braun clock, seven segments, or a dot matrix on the same grid as the timeline. You choose how it counts — it always counts upward, all wheels at once.

A day in pictures

From waking up to the last espresso — the large screens carry the story, the small ones the details. Everything snaps to twelve columns, the way the day snaps to quarter hours.
